# Legal Documents

This section publishes redacted copies of the key legal documents for the Project, mirroring the set previously shared through the Project's data room. Each document is available as a PDF download.

In every document, counterparty identities, signatures, and signing records are removed, and entity names are redacted. Dates and monetary terms are retained. Holder-specific token allocations are not disclosed.

These documents are provided for transparency. They are not offers to sell or solicitations to buy any security, and they are not legal advice.
